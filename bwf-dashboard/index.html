Credit Approval and Internal Controls

Process-mining dashboards for BW Fishing's sales-order event log.

Dashboard Headline metrics — number and value of sales, customers, process variants, average activities per order and the share of orders with credit denied — plus how often each activity was performed.
Process Variants Every distinct path an order took through the process, colour-coded by step type, next to the interactive process map. Isolate or combine variants, click an activity or connection to include or exclude it, and switch the connection labels between case counts and average times.
Transaction Details The event log itself, one row per activity, with employee names and roles and the total value of the orders shown. Sort any column, search, or click an order, activity, employee, role or customer to show just those rows.
Segregation of Duties A cross-tab of activities by employee role: how many times each role performed each activity. Click a cell, activity or role to filter on it.

Filtering

Click any activity or connection on the process map and choose whether to keep only the orders that include it or to exclude them. Clicking any bar on the Dashboard, any value in Transaction Details, or any cell in the Segregation of Duties table keeps only the orders that match (shift-click excludes instead). Each filter appears as a pill in the bar under the tabs and applies to every tab; click a pill to flip it between include and exclude, remove it with its ✕, or clear them all. In Transaction Details the table narrows to the matching rows themselves, so one click shows all the lines for an order, an activity, an employee or a customer.
