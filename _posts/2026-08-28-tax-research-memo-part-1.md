**Tax research memo – Part 1** introduces you to the fundamentals of conducting tax research and preparing tax memos. Tax research memos typically outline the facts, issues, conclusions, and analysis of a particular tax situation. In Part 1, you will learn how to prepare the facts and issues sections of a tax memo for your client, Sarah Johnson. You will also become familiar with authoritative tax sources and how to cite them.

Following Part 1, if assigned, you will complete **Tax research memo – Part 2**, where you will learn how to write the conclusion and analysis sections and complete a final draft of Sarah Johnson’s tax memo.

After drafting the facts and issues, you will receive a pdf copy of your draft and feedback, then you will complete **Tax research memo – Part 1** with a final graded quiz.  
**Learning objectives**

1. Identify the relevant IRC and Treasury Regulation authority for each issue, both with and without generative AI (GenAI) assistance.
2. Write a structured tax analysis applying tax law to the client's specific situation and facts.
3. Cite the primary authority correctly using in-text formatting.
4. Write clear, defensible conclusions that support and answer each issue.
5. Prepare a complete, professionally formatted tax memo.
