This assignment introduces you to the Eight Modes Framework as found within the AI Engagement Model, a research-based framework for using GenAI deliberately and effectively. Rather than treating one approach to AI as universally the best, the model helps you determine the level of engagement that is most appropriate for a given task, both to improve your outputs and to strengthen your critical thinking. You'll explore three tiers of engagement, Passivity, Partnership, and Agency, and examine the risks that arise when GenAI is used without sufficient evaluation or independent thinking, including automation bias and the illusion of understanding. Through instruction, interactive examples, and analysis of your own AI conversations, you'll learn to assess the demands of different tasks, select an appropriate engagement mode, and apply prompting strategies that support that choice. By the end of the assignment, you'll start to develop the skill of matching your level of engagement with GenAI to what a task requires.

**Learning objectives**

1. Explain the Eight Modes Framework and how its engagement modes can be used to select an appropriate level of engagement for a given task.
2. Analyze your own AI interactions to identify patterns in how you engage with GenAI.
3. Recognize your default modes to working with GenAI.
4. Evaluate whether your approach to using GenAI matched the demands of the task and identify opportunities to improve.
5. Develop prompts that effectively apply the appropriate engagement mode for a given task.
