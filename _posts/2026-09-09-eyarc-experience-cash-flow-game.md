Step into the role of a financial analyst preparing the audited statement of cash flows for Global Ventures Corporation. Classify each transaction as an operating, investing, or financing activity (or a noncash adjustment) before time runs out, then review the completed financial statements to catch any errors. Compete with your classmates for the top spot on your course leaderboard.  
  
**Learning objectives**  
1. Classify business cash flows as increases or decreases in operating, investing, and financing activities according to US GAAP.

2. Identify noncash transactions affecting the SCF and reported net income.
