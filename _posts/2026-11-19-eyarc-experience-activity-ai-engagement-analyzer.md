Paste a real conversation you had with a generative AI (GenAI) tool like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini to see how you actually worked with it. The analyzer classifies each of your prompts into one of the eight modes of the AI Engagement Model, shows which tiers you used, tracks how your engagement shifted across the conversation, and suggests ways you could engage differently when a task calls for deeper thinking. Your transcript is never stored; only the report it produces is retained.

**Learning objectives**

1. Identify the eight modes of the AI Engagement Model and the three tiers they form.
2. Recognize your own default modes in working with GenAI.
3. Evaluate whether your approach to using GenAI matched the demands of the task and identify opportunities to improve.

**Suitable courses:** Any course in any area.
